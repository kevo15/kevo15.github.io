Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Shadow Sprinter ("the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by KmGameForge ("we", "us"). The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or other contact details.

Information we collect

Install ID: a random identifier created on your device the first time you open the game. It is not tied to your device hardware, phone number or any account, and it is used only to keep track of your game progress.

Gameplay data: for each level attempt we store the level number, start and end time, duration, result (cleared, quit or died), damage taken and dealt, clones summoned, enemies defeated and stars earned. We also store your unlocked levels, earned titles and totals built from these runs.

App version: the version of the game you are running.

Technical data: our servers are hosted on Google Cloud, which automatically records request information such as IP address and time of request in its server logs. These logs are used only to run and secure the service and are kept for a limited period (typically 30 days).

Settings such as volume, difficulty and controls are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

How we use information

We use the information above to save and restore your progress, show your stats and titles, keep the game running and secure, fix bugs, and understand how levels are played so we can balance them. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Advertising

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. This information is collected by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. On iOS, ads are only personalized if you allow tracking when asked by Apple. If you do not give consent, you may still see non-personalized ads.

We never send your install ID or gameplay data to advertisers.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the game: Google Cloud (server hosting), our cloud database provider (storing gameplay data) and Google AdMob (ads, as described above). We may also disclose information if required by law.

Storage and retention

Gameplay data is stored on servers in the United States. We keep it for as long as you play, until you delete it, or until the game is shut down. Server logs are kept only for a limited period as described above.

Deleting your data

You can erase all gameplay data stored for your install at any time from Settings → Your Data → Delete My Data. This removes it from our servers immediately and starts the game over from level 1.

You can also email kmgameforge@gmail.com with your install ID (shown in Settings → Your Data) and we will delete your data within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the install ID from your device, but data already on our servers stays until it is deleted as described here.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, and to object to or restrict how it is used. Because the game has no accounts, we identify your data by your install ID. To make a request, email us with your install ID. You will not be treated differently for exercising these rights.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, contact kmgameforge@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated, and significant changes will be noted in the game or its store listing.
