Shadow Sprinter

by KmGameForge

Summon your shadow. Your recorded moves become your ally.

A mobile-first 2D action platformer where you summon a shadow clone, record up to 10 seconds of its moves, and watch it repeat them while you play. Take on 30 hand-built levels, race the par time on every stage, and unlock titles as you master each world.

The game

What you'll play

👥

Shadow clone puzzles

Steer your clone while it records, then it repeats every run, jump, climb and strike on its own: holding pressure plates open, riding platforms and hitting enemies from behind while you move on.

🗺️

30 levels in the beta

Hand-built levels across three worlds, from a peaceful dojo through a corrupted village and deep into the forest.

⏱️

Race the par time

Every level has a par time. Finishing is just the start; mastery means beating the clock.

🏅

32 titles to unlock

Earn titles like Initiate and Village Runner by clearing levels and hitting par times.

🥷

Physics-based movement

Tight running and jumping with real-time collision and enemy AI.
